# 04 — The Paper (exact)

## What kind of paper

An **empirical study / measurement paper** (quantitative benchmark study with a reproducible
harness). NOT a novel-technique paper. Its three claims:

1. Quantified generational decay of prompt-enrichment and repair-loop benefits (RQ1–RQ3).
2. A residual-risk catalog: which CWE categories still slip through full scaffolding on
   2026 models (RQ4).
3. **First measurement of "repair inflation"** — how much iterative scanner-feedback repair
   overstates success when functional correctness is unchecked (RQ5).

## Working titles (pick at submission time)

1. *"Does Security Scaffolding Still Matter? Prompt Enrichment and Iterative Repair Across LLM Generations"*
2. *"When Repair Lies: Measuring Secure-but-Broken Code in LLM Repair Loops Across Model Generations"*
3. *"LeBlanc: Quantifying the Generational Gap in Secure LLM Code Generation"*

## Section skeleton (write to this, in this order)

1. **Introduction** — the 2022 "40% vulnerable" era → 2026 models pass security trivia →
   so is scaffolding dead? Contributions = 3 claims above.
2. **Background & Related Work** — the 8-paper lit review, reused honestly:
   SecuCoGen (enrichment works, single-turn), Guiding-AI-to-Fix (feedback helps, no loop),
   HexaCoder (oracle-guided repair prompts), CODEGUARD+ (secure-pass@k — our RQ5 lens),
   CodeSecEval (executable eval), DOMAINEVAL (domain difficulty), Copilot replication
   (generational drift), DiSCo/LPO (the fine-tuning road we deliberately don't take).
3. **The LeBlanc Pipeline** — Engines A–D, one diagram (reuse VAPT report TikZ).
4. **Experimental Design** — dataset (02), fleet & buckets (03), judge (03), stats (03).
5. **Results** — one subsection per RQ. RQ4 heatmap and RQ5 inflation chart are the money figures.
6. **Threats to Validity** — static-analysis FPs (report κ + FP rate), 3-iteration cap,
   Python-only, generation buckets partly confounded with size/vendor, smoke tests ≠ full
   functional correctness.
7. **Conclusion** — practitioner guidance: when scaffolding is worth it (small/local models,
   specific categories) and when it's theater.

## Writing rules

- Every number in the paper is generated by `analysis/rq_analysis.ipynb` from the DB. No exceptions.
- Negative/null results (e.g., "enrichment does nothing on G3") are **headline findings**, not
  embarrassments — the paper is framed so null results are still a story.
- Limitations section is written FIRST, not last (keeps claims honest while writing results).
